Sales Benefits Summary

How to position Thales value for Snowflake customers

This summary turns the Thales and Snowflake solution brief into a rep-friendly story: why customers buy, what business problems the joint offering solves, and how to connect the right Thales capability to the right Snowflake conversation.

The top-line message

Snowflake already delivers strong built-in security. Thales helps customers go further when they need externalized key control, stronger separation of duties, data-centric protection, or better coverage across hybrid and multi-cloud environments.

  • Protect sensitive data without slowing the business. Customers can support analytics, AI, application development, and sharing while adding stronger control around the most sensitive information.
  • Give security teams more control. Thales lets customers manage keys, policies, secrets, and data protection options outside the core data platform when business or regulatory requirements call for it.
  • Meet sovereignty and compliance pressure. External key management and customer-controlled protection help organizations demonstrate that they retain meaningful control over critical data assets.
  • Fit the way enterprises actually operate. The joint story works across SaaS, hybrid cloud, multi-cloud, and containerized Snowflake workloads.
Who this resonates with

CISOs, data platform owners, security architects, privacy leaders, compliance teams, and cloud engineering leads.

Common triggers

AI adoption, cross-border data concerns, audit findings, key ownership requirements, secrets sprawl, or higher-value data moving into Snowflake.

What reps should listen for

"We need customer-managed keys," "our regulators want stronger control," "we are sharing more sensitive data," or "we need better DevSecOps hygiene."

Simple positioning

Thales adds defense in depth around Snowflake so customers can keep moving fast without giving up control of sensitive data, keys, and secrets.

Thales Snowflake Sales Benefits Summary 1
Customer Value Drivers

Why customers look beyond native controls

The opportunity is strongest when a Snowflake customer needs security controls that are broader, more externalized, or easier to prove to auditors and regulators.

Control over encryption keys

Many enterprises want logical control of keys even when workloads run in SaaS or public cloud environments. That lets them show that access to data is not controlled by the platform provider alone.

Data sovereignty and privacy

Cross-border rules, sector regulations, and internal governance standards often push buyers to separate data use from key ownership and to keep sensitive controls under their own authority.

Protection for high-value data

As Snowflake becomes home to customer records, payment data, identity numbers, and AI-ready datasets, buyers look for stronger protection on the most critical fields and workflows.

Hybrid and multi-cloud consistency

Security teams rarely manage one platform in one cloud. Thales helps them use common controls and governance patterns across broader environments, not only within a single service boundary.

DevSecOps acceleration

Teams want developers and data engineers to move quickly without hard-coding secrets or creating manual security work. Automated secrets, rotation, and policy controls reduce friction while improving hygiene.

Auditability and reporting

Customers increasingly need to prove not just that protection exists, but that it is monitored, governed, and aligned to policy. That is especially important for shared data and AI initiatives.

Business conversation flow

1

Start with risk

Ask what sensitive data is moving into Snowflake and what would happen if key custody, secrets, or privileged access were questioned.

2

Surface the trigger

Look for compliance pressure, privacy commitments, cloud migration, AI expansion, or the need to protect shared data products.

3

Map to control

Connect the trigger to the right Thales answer: key ownership, encryption or tokenization, secrets management, or data security visibility.

4

Land the outcome

Frame success as faster data use with stronger proof of control, lower compliance friction, and a safer path to AI and collaboration.

Rep sound bite

"Snowflake helps you move data and AI initiatives faster. Thales helps you keep control of the keys, secrets, and sensitive data protections that make those initiatives safe to scale."

Use this when the buyer understands Snowflake well but has not yet connected security architecture to business value.

Rep Playbook

Use cases, qualification cues, and talk tracks

High-probability use cases

  • Financial services: customers want stronger control over payment data, account data, and encryption keys while expanding data sharing and analytics.
  • Healthcare and public sector: privacy, residency, and audit concerns create demand for customer-controlled protection and stronger proof of governance.
  • Global enterprises: sovereignty and cross-border data handling make external key management especially compelling.
  • AI and data product teams: sensitive data is being prepared for broader internal and partner use, raising the need for tokenization, masking, and secrets automation.
  • Modern platform teams: containerized Snowpark services create a natural conversation about secure in-platform processing.
Qualification question

Who owns the decision for key custody and data protection policy today: the cloud platform team, the security team, or compliance leadership?

Qualification question

Are you being asked to prove customer control of keys or sensitive data access for regulators, auditors, or internal governance?

Qualification question

What sensitive data types are moving into Snowflake now: payment data, national IDs, health data, employee records, or proprietary data products?

Qualification question

Where are secrets, tokens, and service credentials currently managed for Snowflake-connected workloads and automation?

If the buyer says

"Snowflake already secures data."

Agree first, then pivot: Thales is not a replacement for native security. It is an added layer for customers who need external key control, stronger sovereignty posture, or broader data-centric security across environments.

If the buyer says

"We need proof for auditors."

Lead with customer-managed keys, centralized policy, discovery, monitoring, and reporting. Position Thales as a way to make security control easier to demonstrate, not just easier to claim.

If the buyer says

"We are moving fast on AI."

Connect Thales to safer AI enablement: protect sensitive data, reduce secrets sprawl, and keep stronger governance in place as more teams and models touch the data estate.

If the buyer says

"We operate in multiple clouds."

Focus on consistency. Thales gives security teams common control patterns that extend across hybrid and multi-cloud environments instead of creating a separate approach for every platform.

Good discovery pivot: "When you say control, do you mean operational convenience, or do you mean provable customer ownership of keys and sensitive data protections?"

Closing Guidance

How to land the business value with an executive buyer

Executive summary pitch

Thales helps Snowflake customers strengthen control over sensitive data without giving up the speed and scale of the AI Data Cloud. The value is simple: more choice in how data is protected, stronger ownership of keys and secrets, and a clearer path to compliance, sovereignty, and secure growth.

For executive audiences, keep the message centered on risk reduction, regulator confidence, and enabling the business to expand analytics, AI, and data sharing responsibly.

30-second talk track

"If Snowflake is becoming a more strategic home for your most valuable data, Thales helps you add the controls that matter most to security and compliance teams: customer-managed keys, stronger data protection, secure secrets handling, and better visibility across the broader data estate."

Business outcomes to emphasize

  • Reduced exposure of sensitive data
  • Stronger sovereignty posture
  • Easier compliance conversations
  • Safer AI and analytics expansion

Best next-step offers

  • Architecture workshop on key ownership strategy
  • Use-case review for BYOE or tokenization
  • Secrets management assessment
  • Discovery session on data security visibility gaps

What not to do

  • Do not frame the story as fear-only security selling.
  • Do not imply Snowflake security is insufficient by default.
  • Do not overload the buyer with every Thales module at once.
  • Do not skip the regulatory or sovereignty angle when it is clearly present.
